# Vox Cambridge Reverb (V1031/V1032) — Electronics Rebuild

Complete electronics rebuild of a Vox Cambridge Reverb solid-state guitar amplifier:
the original electronics are gutted and replaced with modern parts while preserving
the original functionality, the front/rear panel layout (no new holes), and the
chassis. Guitar-frequency and 10″ alnico-speaker optimized throughout.

> **Honesty up front:** the Part 1–10 design docs were **reconstructed** from a prior
> conversation (see `docs/PROVENANCE.md`); some values were gap-filled. The KiCad
> project, BOM, and ngspice sims are **generated and tool-verified** (ERC/DRC/SPICE
> all pass), but the board is **placed, not routed**, and a few stages are designed
> substitutes. Treat the signal chain and power section as *validated design intent*,
> and re-verify part numbers/values before ordering or building.

## Core design decisions

| Block | Replacement |
|-------|-------------|
| Power amp | LM1875T (replaces germanium push-pull; eliminates driver transformer T2) |
| Preamp JFETs | MMBF5457 (SOT-23: direct on the SMD variant, on an adapter for THT) or J113 (2N5457 discontinued) — **availability watch**; pins 1 D / 2 S / 3 G (errata #18) |
| Reverb / tremolo | TL072 ×2 (no transformer T3): IC1 = tank driver + **active wet/dry summer**; IC2 = tremolo LFO + **output buffer** |
| Reverb pan | Accutronics 4FB2A1C (~1475 Ω input) — 8 Ω 4AB3C1B is **incompatible** |
| Tremolo modulation | Xvive VTL5C1 or DIY LED/LDR optocoupler, driven by a TL072 Wien LFO — **symmetric, dual-gang 250 k speed pot, 0.6–10.6 Hz** (errata #19: the recovered single-arm pot could not oscillate at tremolo rates); LED-limited LFO + **2N3904 emitter-follower LED driver, 15 dB depth** (errata #22: as drawn the vactrol LED ran at 7 mA DC — a fixed −20 dB pad with 1.7 dB of wobble) |
| Power | LM317T-regulated +17 V rail with **`C_adj`** and an RC-decoupled **`+17V_PRE`** rail for the JFET stages (errata #21: the regulator's noise sat 23 dB above the JFET floor); split `VBIAS_R`/`VBIAS_T`/`VBIAS_3` mid-rails; KBP410G bridge; `VREG_IN` RC pre-filter; **bypassed LM1875 bias divider** (errata #21: as drawn it fed rail ripple ×23 to the speaker — 0.4 Vrms of hum) |
| Tone | **Bass + Treble** (the panel's pots) on a passive James / Thomas-Vox network with a TL074 buffer + make-up (IC3), 470 pF chime cap as a bright cap, and a **MID CUT toggle** (−9.6 dB @ ~800 Hz, broad) in the line-reverse switch hole — errata #20 (the recovered 470 pF-only coupling passed no bass at all) |

## What's verified (all via real tools, in CI-able commands)
- **Schematic:** generated by `kicad/gen/gen_kicad.py` → 8 hierarchical sheets, **ERC 0 violations** (KiCad 8.0.9). 150 components + 28 test points.
- **PCB:** `kicad/gen/gen_pcb.py` places every footprint into the **Part 5 floor-plan zones** (datasheet-derived LM1875 / KBP bridge footprints, real can sizes, Part 4 wire pads, mounting holes; **0 DRC errors, 0 warnings before routing**), plus a **labelled bench test-point strip per zone** (28 `TP_*`: rails, JFET drains, mid-rail refs, every inter-stage node, PA output, a GND pin per zone), then `kicad/gen/route_board.py` **autoroutes headlessly** with Freerouting. **218 of 226 connections routed (incl. the 28 test points), 0 DRC errors, 0 rule hits** on the 4-layer board — outer layers carry escapes only (1.7 m top, 1.4 m bottom), the long runs sit on In2 under the GND plane (2.3 m), 48 vias + 34 stitching vias; the eight open links are listed in the notes. The board packs tighter since the Bass/Treble stack (errata #20) — the previous placement reached 167/168. See `kicad/PCB-NOTES.md`.
- **Mixed SMD/THT variant** (`kicad/smd/`, **170×100 mm** — grown from Part 7's 155×90 safe-bet with the 4-layer / keepout regime, errata #24; still smaller than the 190×115 original): same schematic through a second footprint profile — 90 small parts on 0805/1206/1210/SMA/SOD-123/SOT-23, power parts / electrolytics / socketed op-amps / trim resistors / connectors stay THT. **216 of 226 connections routed, 1 DRC error** (a starved thermal on the plane), rules clean, on the 170×100 4-layer board; the ten open links (a power-amp cluster and the tank drive) are listed in the notes. JLCPCB-assembly BOM + position file in `production/smd/`.
- **BOM:** generated from the schematic by `kicad/gen/gen_bom.py` (1:1 with the netlist, no drift) → `bom/bom.csv` + `bom/bom-grouped.csv` (THT) and `bom/bom-smd*.csv` (variant).
- **Noise / hum / crosstalk / thermal** (`docs/06c-…`): `.noise` of the whole front end with the regulator's own noise (EIN 37 → 2.7 µV, SNR at 12 W 34 → 57 dB after errata #21), rail-ripple paths (hum 408 mV → 0.3 mV at the speaker), a vactrol-level tremolo model (depth 1.7 → 15 dB, errata #22), a geometric **crosstalk audit** of both routed boards feeding two routing clearance rules, and an electro-thermal LM1875 model (2.5 K/W sink: Tj 97 °C at 40 °C ambient, worst-case sine).
- **Four layers, no routes through parts** (errata #24): both boards are now 4-layer — solid In1 GND plane under the component layer, In2 inner signal layer, B.Cu GND pour with perimeter stitching vias — every part carries keepout areas between its pins, nets are classed by impedance (`HiZ` 0.3 mm / `Default` / `Power` / `HighCurrent`) with clearance rules between the classes (0.6 mm on a 0.21 mm plane spacing couples like 2 mm did before), and RC stoppers sit at the two antenna inputs.
- **JLCPCB:** both projects carry JLC's design limits as DRC constraints (two footprints were under the annular-ring minimum and are fixed), the SMD board has fiducials, every SMD capacitor is a basic-library 1206, optional parts are DNP — 7 → 3 extended part types, assembly ≈ $20 for five boards (`kicad/gen/jlc_cost.py`).
- **SPICE:** `spice/run_all.sh` runs 12 ngspice block checks (preamp bias, reverb driver, MRB, tremolo LFO, power amp, **rail-aware** reverb summer, **rail-aware** tone stack, Class-A stage, front-end noise, hum paths, tremolo depth, LM1875 thermal) **plus 6 parameter sweeps** (JFET bias vs R_s across the datasheet Idss spread, Bass / Treble pots + mid-cut switch, LFO speed pot — recovered and fixed networks, Class-A bias, power-amp headroom vs mains ±10 %) and saves every result table to **`spice/results/`** (committed evidence).

## Honest engineering notes (from the audit/roast passes — see `docs/roast-2026-06-16.md`)
- 🔴 **Output power ≈ 12 W, not 18 W.** A single 33.5 V rail can't swing the 17 V peak that 18 W into 8 Ω needs; the LM1875 ceilings at ~12 W clean (~1.8 dB down). Fine for a 10″ combo.
- 🔴 **Fit a MAINS (primary) fuse** — the design's other fuse is only on the secondary. Required for safety.
- ⚠️ **Chassis fit:** the board is **190×115 mm** (matches the original 25-5274-2). Real (text-less) packing is **~40 %** there (THT parts) and **~54 %** for the SMD-profile parts on the Part 7 155×90 "safe-bet" (the THT parts would need 68 % of it) — the earlier 53 % / 90 % figures counted hidden text. Measure the real chassis before committing to a size (errata #9); the placer rebalances to any outline.

## Key constraints
- No new holes in original panels; all improvements internal only.
- MRB hardwired to 600 Hz (original spec); tremolo rate LED is PCB-mounted (diagnostic).
- Original 6-pin DIN footswitch connector and pedal retained.

## Tools
- **EDA / verification:** KiCad 8 (`kicad-cli` ERC/DRC). `kicad/gen/check.sh tht|smd` regenerates a profile and writes ERC/DRC reports to `kicad/reports/` (add `--no-regen` to verify a routed board); route with `python3 kicad/gen/route_board.py` (Freerouting 1.9.0 jar + Java + xvfb, see the script)
- **Datasheets:** `datasheets/` holds the manufacturer PDFs the footprints and pinouts were taken from, with a manifest (`SOURCES.md`: file, revision, checksum, which decision it backs)
- **Simulation:** ngspice (`spice/run_all.sh`); LTspice netlist kept for the power amp
- **PCB fab / sourcing:** JLCPCB · Mouser / Digikey
