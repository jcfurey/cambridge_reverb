# Vox Cambridge Reverb (V1031/V1032) — Electronics Rebuild

Complete electronics rebuild of a Vox Cambridge Reverb solid-state guitar amplifier:
the original electronics are gutted and replaced with modern parts while preserving
the original functionality, the front/rear panel layout (no new holes), and the
chassis. Guitar-frequency and 10″ alnico-speaker optimized throughout.

> **Honesty up front:** the Part 1–10 design docs were **reconstructed** from a prior
> conversation (see `docs/PROVENANCE.md`); some values were gap-filled, and a few stages
> are designed substitutes. The KiCad project, BOM, order package and ngspice sims are
> **generated and tool-verified** — both boards are fully routed with 0 DRC errors, and
> `kicad/gen/verify.py` checks them pin for pin against the schematic and the
> datasheets. **Nothing has been built or measured.** A datasheet audit and new simulations
> found six faults in November 2026 that ERC and DRC could not see (errata #25–#30), and
> measuring the copper found a slotted ground plane and blind vias (errata #31);
> expect the bench to find more. Order one batch, bring it up on a series lamp.

## Core design decisions

| Block | Replacement |
|-------|-------------|
| Power amp | LM1875T (replaces germanium push-pull; eliminates driver transformer T2) |
| Preamp JFETs | MMBF5457 (SOT-23: direct on the SMD variant, on an adapter for THT) or J113 (2N5457 discontinued) — **availability watch**; pins 1 D / 2 S / 3 G (errata #18) |
| Reverb / tremolo | TL072 ×2 (no transformer T3): IC1 = tank driver + **active wet/dry summer**; IC2 = tremolo LFO + **output buffer** |
| Reverb pan | Accutronics 4FB2A1C (~1475 Ω input) — 8 Ω 4AB3C1B is **incompatible** |
| Tremolo modulation | Xvive VTL5C1 or DIY LED/LDR optocoupler (axial part, **pad 1 = LED cathode** — errata #25), driven by a TL072 Wien LFO — **symmetric, dual-gang 250 k speed pot, 0.6–10.6 Hz** (errata #19: the recovered single-arm pot could not oscillate at tremolo rates); LED-limited LFO + **2N3904 emitter-follower LED driver, 14 dB depth** (errata #22: as drawn the vactrol LED ran at 7 mA DC — a fixed −20 dB pad with 1.7 dB of wobble) |
| Footswitch | Three contacts to ground on the original DIN connector: reverb off, tremolo off, **MRB on** — the MRB tank returns to ground *through* the pedal, so with no pedal the amp is flat (errata #27: the lines used to end on pull-downs and the 577 Hz band-pass was permanently in circuit) |
| Preamp gain | Two JFET stages, source-bypass caps `C_s1` / `C_s2` **optional and off by default**: 21 dB, clean to ~0.3 V pk; fitted they give 33 / 45 dB and an overdriven second stage (errata #28) |
| Power | Centre-tapped secondary into the KBP410G as a **two-diode full-wave** (link `JP_BR` open; fit it only for a single 24 V winding — errata #26: CT and the bridge's "−" were both grounded, a short across each half-winding); LM317T-regulated +17 V rail with **`C_adj`** and an RC-decoupled **`+17V_PRE`** rail for the JFET stages (errata #21: the regulator's noise sat 23 dB above the JFET floor); split `VBIAS_R`/`VBIAS_T`/`VBIAS_3` mid-rails; `VREG_IN` RC pre-filter; **bypassed LM1875 bias divider** (errata #21: as drawn it fed rail ripple ×23 to the speaker — 0.4 Vrms of hum) |
| Tone | **Bass + Treble** (the panel's pots) on a passive James / Thomas-Vox network with a TL074 buffer + make-up (IC3), 470 pF chime cap as a bright cap, and a **MID CUT toggle** (−9.6 dB @ ~800 Hz, broad) in the line-reverse switch hole — errata #20 (the recovered 470 pF-only coupling passed no bass at all) |

## What's verified (all via real tools — `kicad/gen/build.sh smd`, `kicad/gen/verify.py smd`)
- **One gate:** `kicad/gen/verify.py` — **75 of 75 checks** on the SMD board, 66 of 66 on the THT board (`kicad/reports/verify-*.txt`). It reads the finished files, not the generator's data: ERC, DRC, schematic-to-board parity pin for pin, a pin-function audit against the datasheets, net-level electrical assertions (clamp-diode direction, rectifier topology, electrolytic polarity at the DC operating point, supply pins, pot directions, footswitch), silkscreen, JLCPCB's published limits, the star-ground topology and a solve of the return currents in the routed copper, the ground-system ("RF") rules measured on the finished copper, order-package consistency, and the SPICE suite with a schematic-to-netlist value cross-check.
- **Schematic:** generated by `kicad/gen/gen_kicad.py` → 8 hierarchical sheets, **ERC 0 violations** (KiCad 8.0.9). SMD build: 157 components + 29 test points, 88 nets (the THT build has 181 parts: one 68 nF and one 3 k where the SMD board uses JLCPCB-basic multiples).
- **The JLCPCB board** (`kicad/smd/`, **170 × 105 mm**, 4 layers): **251 of 251 connections routed, 0 DRC errors, 0 warnings** with JLCPCB's limits as constraints. 99 small parts (0805 / 1206 / SMA / SOT-23) are placed by JLCPCB; 37 through-hole parts — power devices, electrolytics, socketed op-amps, the bench-trimmed resistors, the bright cap — are soldered by hand. Every part is labelled on the silkscreen and every wiring pad says what it is for.
- **Star ground** (errata #32, `kicad/gen/ground_audit.py` → `kicad/reports/ground-*.md`): the planes are signal ground only; transformer centre tap, reservoir, speaker return and the LM1875's ground pin are separate routed nets that meet the planes through one 0 Ω link at the speaker's return terminal. Solving the routed copper as a resistor network puts the charging-pulse buzz at the speaker at **13 µV** at idle (the single-net board of the day before: 4.4 mV, 31 dBA at 1 m) and no speaker current in the planes. LM1875 decoupling and Zobel sit 20–15 mm from its pins (were 62–66 mm).
- **Power-amp stability** (errata #35, `spice/ac_pa_stability.cir`): the roll-off capacitor across the LM1875's feedback resistor is gone — it took the noise gain of a gain-≥-10 amplifier to 1 (−9° / −1° of phase margin in a three-pole model); the 7.7 kHz roll-off is an RC at the input, +68° / +76°.
- **Ground system** (errata #31, `kicad/gen/rf_audit.py` → `kicad/reports/rf-*.md`): signal / **solid ground** / signal / **solid ground**, nothing routed on a plane, each signal layer 0.21 mm from its own plane; plane openings sized by the holes so no slot forms along a row of pins (3 openings over 5 mm in the inner plane, was 31); copper 0.5 mm back from the edge with a ground via fence every 5 mm; a via beside every SMD ground pad and 337 ground vias in all, no point further than 13 mm from one; top pour 0.5 mm from every trace and 1.0 mm from high-impedance nets. Stray capacitance of every sensitive net is tabulated: the lowest corner is `TONE_OUT`, 25 pF → 101 kHz. All vias are plain through vias.
- **Order package** (`production/smd/`, built by `kicad/gen/fab_jlcpcb.py`): Gerber + drill zip, assembly BOM with **an LCSC part number on every one of its 26 lines** (each looked up in JLCPCB's library — stock, class, package; `kicad/gen/jlc_parts.py --check` re-reads them), pick-and-place file in JLCPCB's format with the SOT-23 rotation offset, assembly drawing. 20 basic / 3 preferred / **3 extended** part types (were 14 — errata #36): assembly fees ≈ $21 for five boards, ≈ $45 with the parts (`kicad/gen/jlc_cost.py`).
- **THT board** (`kicad/`, 190 × 115 mm, the original outline): same circuit, same stack, same star ground, all through-hole, **245 of 245 routed, 0 DRC errors, 0 warnings**; ground-return hum 31 µV (was 7.4 mV).
- **Routing:** `kicad/gen/route_board.py` autoroutes headlessly with Freerouting on the two signal layers; `kicad/gen/finish_routes.py`, a small maze router that applies the project's rules as written, closes the 7–13 links Freerouting leaves (signal-level branches of power nets, the tank drive), re-ordering and starting over if one finds no path; `kicad/gen/stitch.py` then stitches the grounds. Keepout areas between every part's pins, nets classed by impedance, RC stoppers at the two antenna inputs (errata #24). See `kicad/PCB-NOTES.md`.
- **BOM:** generated from the schematic by `kicad/gen/gen_bom.py` (1:1 with the netlist, no drift) → `bom/bom.csv` + `bom/bom-grouped.csv` (THT) and `bom/bom-smd*.csv` (variant).
- **Noise / hum / crosstalk / thermal** (`docs/06c-…`): `.noise` of the whole front end with the regulator's own noise (EIN 37 → 2.7 µV, SNR at 12 W 34 → 57 dB after errata #21), rail-ripple paths (hum 408 mV → 0.3 mV at the speaker), a vactrol-level tremolo model, a geometric **crosstalk audit** of both routed boards, and an electro-thermal LM1875 model (2.5 K/W sink: Tj 97 °C at 40 °C ambient, worst-case sine).
- **SPICE:** `spice/run_all.sh` runs 15 ngspice block checks (preamp bias, reverb driver, MRB engaged / bypassed, tremolo LFO, power amp and its **loop stability**, **rail-aware** reverb summer and its **stability against stray capacitance**, **rail-aware** tone stack, Class-A stage, front-end noise, hum paths, tremolo depth with the footswitch, LM1875 thermal, **preamp headroom at pickup levels**) **plus 6 parameter sweeps** and saves every result table to **`spice/results/`** (committed evidence).

## Honest engineering notes (from the audit/roast passes — see `docs/roast-2026-06-16.md`)
- 🔴 **Output power ≈ 12 W, not 18 W.** A single 33.5 V rail can't swing the 17 V peak that 18 W into 8 Ω needs; the LM1875 ceilings at ~12 W clean (~1.8 dB down). Fine for a 10″ combo.
- 🔴 **Fit a MAINS (primary) fuse** — the design's other fuse is only on the secondary. Required for safety.
- 🔴 **Know your transformer before you wire it** (errata #26): centre tap to `CT` with `JP_BR` open, *or* a single winding with `JP_BR` fitted and `CT` empty.
- ⚠️ **Chassis fit:** the THT board is **190×115 mm** (the original 25-5274-2 outline), the JLCPCB board **170×105 mm**. Nobody has measured the actual chassis (errata #9); set `BW, BH` in `gen_pcb.py` and run `build.sh` if it disagrees — the placer and both routers adapt.

## Key constraints
- No new holes in original panels; all improvements internal only.
- MRB tuned to 600 Hz (original spec), switched by the footswitch; tremolo rate LED is PCB-mounted (diagnostic).
- Original 6-pin DIN footswitch connector and pedal retained (one contact to ground per effect).

## Tools
- **EDA / verification:** KiCad 8.0.9 (`kicad-cli`, `pcbnew` Python). `kicad/gen/build.sh smd|tht` rebuilds a board end to end (generate → Freerouting 1.9.0 → `finish_routes.py` → reports → order package → verify; needs Java, the Freerouting jar, xvfb, numpy, scipy, ngspice); `kicad/gen/verify.py smd|tht` checks an existing build
- **Datasheets:** `datasheets/` holds the manufacturer PDFs the footprints and pinouts were taken from, with a manifest (`SOURCES.md`: file, revision, checksum, which decision it backs)
- **Simulation:** ngspice (`spice/run_all.sh`); LTspice netlist kept for the power amp
- **PCB fab / sourcing:** JLCPCB · Mouser / Digikey
